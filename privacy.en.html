Privacy Policy

Eottabeoryeo · Developer: Yuyeon · Last updated: October 23, 2026
This is an English translation of the Korean privacy policy. If the English and Korean versions differ, the Korean version applies.
Your searches stay on your device. Ad information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners.

Android 1.1.2 (4) is a version without an ad SDK and without internet permission. From Android 1.2.0 (5) the app is a banner-supported edition, and later versions that keep the same way of handling banner information are also covered by this notice. This policy applies to the information actually handled by the version you installed. Please check the description below that matches your installed version. In both editions the item data is included in the app, so you can search without internet.

1. Scope and information we handle

Eottabeoryeo is an app for looking up waste-sorting information in Korea without signing up. The app does not ask for your name, contact details, photos or precise location as input for any feature. Search terms may contain personal content, so please do not enter sensitive information.

The earlier Android 1.1.2 (4) has no real ad SDK, payments or internet permission, and does not send search terms or search history to any external server. In the banner-supported edition that applies from Android 1.2.0 (5), external communication and ad information processing are added for ads and privacy-choice messages. This does not mean the whole app collects no personal information or sends nothing outside.

2. Search history and storage on your device

Recent searches and words that returned no results are stored only on your device. We do not send search history to the developer's servers, to ad request search keywords, to ad content addresses, to analytics services or to error logs. The developer cannot see the search history on your device. You can delete recent searches and no-result words with "Clear recent searches" in the app.

Records from earlier versions, such as the number of app openings and support prompts, are no longer used in the new version, but they are not deleted automatically when you update. The ad and consent SDKs may keep information on your device storage for your consent choices and for ads to work. "Clear recent searches" does not delete everything the SDKs store or everything Google and advertising partners have already processed.

This banner edition does not ask for or store your date of birth, age or age range in the app. Only your ad on/off choice and your display language choice (Korean/English) are kept in the app settings on your device, separately from your search history. If an earlier age setting remains on the device, the app keeps the previous ad-blocking choice and removes the age range value.

Android 1.1.2 (4) and the new 1.2.0 (5) are set to exclude app data such as search history from automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. We have not verified actual behavior on every device and operating system or the existence of past backups, and this update does not delete device or cloud backups made earlier.

The earlier Android 1.0 (1) allowed operating system app data backup. Depending on your device, operating system and settings, app data may have been included in cloud backup or device-to-device transfer, and we have not checked whether any backup actually took place. The cautions about backups of earlier versions still apply.

3. Permissions and external communication of the banner edition

The earlier 1.1.2 (4) does not request internet, advertising ID, location, camera or contacts permission. The new 1.2.0 (5) uses internet and network state checks for ad communication. It does not include the Android advertising ID access permission. It does not add location, camera or contacts permissions. Without internet, ads cannot load, but item search using the data included in the app still works.

The item search screen uses the data included in the app and is restricted from automatically loading external web resources. We do not add a developer search collection server, Firebase Analytics or any external analytics SDK. If you choose an email or external link, your email app or browser opens and further communication follows that service's policy.

4. Google AdMob banner ads and purpose of processing

The new 1.2.0 (5) is a banner edition that uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Banners appear in an area separate from search and guidance content, and the app does not use interstitial ads, app-open ads, rewarded ads or exit-dialog ads. Video types are also excluded in the banner ad unit. Text, image and rich media banners may include animated or interactive creatives.

To deliver, measure and analyze ads and to prevent fraud such as invalid clicks, Google and permitted advertising partners may collect, use and share the following information.

  • Your IP address and the approximate location inferred from it. This does not mean the app requests precise location permission such as GPS.
  • Device or app identifiers such as an app set ID. This app excludes the Android advertising ID access permission and sets the protection handling described in section 7 below. Depending on the SDK and device state, other account or app related identifiers may be processed.
  • App and ad interaction information such as app launches, ad impressions and reactions.
  • Performance and diagnostic information such as app and ad SDK start time, response delays, freezes and energy use.

Information collected by the SDK is encrypted in transit with TLS. Encryption or an advertising partner's compliance with its policies is not a guarantee against hacking or leaks. For handling, retention and deletion standards, please see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The permitted partners and each company's information use are described in the Google ad partners guide and in the consent screen where applicable.

5. Your privacy choices and changes

The banner edition uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check your regional consent status and show the required privacy choice messages. In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are requested after the required steps. The scope for Google and permitted advertising partners follows your choices and regional rules. A partner choice setting in one region does not mean that all third-party information receipt is blocked worldwide.

When you need to change your privacy choices, you can open "Ad privacy settings" in the app to turn ads on or off and view Google consent and privacy information. While consent information is loading or the consent form is showing, please wait a moment and open it again so that other settings windows do not overlap. Resetting or deleting the advertising ID in Android settings is an operating-system-wide choice and does not mean this app uses the advertising ID. Available features differ by Android version. Non-personalized ads may still use your IP address, some identifiers or device storage for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention, so they do not mean that no personal information is collected.

The developer does not store per-user advertising identifier records on a separate server and does not provide a feature to delete ad information directly. Retention periods and deletion or control methods for Google and advertising partners follow each company's policy and the user-facing settings. You can clear search history in the app, and you can delete the app's data on your device by uninstalling the app or by clearing app data in the operating system. We do not guarantee that past backups or information already processed externally will be deleted.

If you turn ads off, the current banner is removed and new ad requests from the app code are blocked. This is not a feature for taking back requests or information already passed to Google or partners. There is no guarantee that all internal processing by an SDK that has already started ends immediately. After you fully close the app and start it again, the app code does not start a UMP consent request, ad SDK initialization or ad requests, according to your saved choice to turn ads off.

6. Contact and retention

When you press a contact button, your email app opens and you decide whether to send. A message you send yourself may include what you wrote and the email address for our reply. The developer uses it to answer your inquiry and for necessary follow-up, and deletes content that is no longer needed once the purpose is achieved. Where a legal retention obligation applies, that period is followed. Please include only the necessary content in your inquiry.

7. Age information and children's personal information

This banner edition does not ask for or store your date of birth, age or age range. For ad requests, regardless of who the user is, we set Google's enhanced protection handling (CHILD) and limit ad creatives to the rating suitable for all users (G). Google states that for ad requests set to CHILD, personalized ads, remarketing and third-party ad company requests are disabled and the Android advertising ID is not sent. It does not mean confirmation or a claim that a user is a child, blocking of all information handling by all partners, or that no personal information is collected. Google's regional consent process still applies, and the app does not skip that process by designating all users as under the age of consent. Creative ratings and protection signals do not remove all SDK information handling or replace required consent and legal guardian consent. The SDK's startup app-settings checks and the like exist, and there is no guarantee that there is never any device communication even when ads are turned off. If you believe a child's information has been handled inappropriately, please tell us at the contact below.

8. Accuracy of information and policy changes

Waste sorting methods are based on the public data of the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment, and some items are supplemented with public standards such as the Ministry of Environment recycling guideline. Rules can differ by municipality and by the condition of the item, so please check your local community center, your local government website and the product label.

If the version or the way information is handled changes, we update this page and the in-app policy. This policy describes information handling by version and does not guarantee actual ad impressions or revenue.
